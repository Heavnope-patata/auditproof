1 - Compile policy Policy Compiler -> structured policy + policyHash
Plain-English rule goes through Claude and becomes a structured policy the circuit can read. Units are normalized (percent to basis points, time to UTC seconds), ambiguities take the strictest interpretation and are flagged for officer review.
2 - Three independent witnesses each checks its own fact locally, returns only a signed 0/1
3 - Generate zero-knowledge proof circom + Groth16
Circuit checks three EdDSA signatures, that each signature is bound to this policy and this trade, that the reasoning commitment matches the agent's actual reasoning, then outputs a single bit.
4 - Regulator verifies off chain, no party data needed
The regulator only has the verification key, public signals, and the witness registry. They can confirm the verdict; they never see amounts, NAV, sanctions content, or strategy source.
Raw data disclosed to the agent or to the chain during this run: zero records.
Each of the three institutions returns only one signed 0/1 plus the trade fingerprint it computed itself. Portfolio NAV, sanctions list, market data feed, and the agent's quant strategy source never leave their own processes.
